Prompt final:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: premium TOP3D website hero illustration, not an actual commercial product photograph. Create a photorealistic CGI studio composition of three unbranded 3D printer filament spools, a large vivid turquoise/cyan filament spool upright in the foreground at a three-quarter angle, a smaller deep violet spool reclining behind on the right, and dark charcoal spool behind on the left. The spool frames are sophisticated matte black with circular openings and fine engineering details. Extremely fine densely wound filament lines, tactile realistic plastic, dramatic softbox lighting and cyan rim light. Ground plane seamless almost black charcoal #0b0e12, subtle contact shadows, no gradients or fog. Composition square with objects entirely in frame and generous dark margins, centered cluster filling 75% of frame. Premium industrial product advertising, physically plausible dimensions and hollow central axle, no text, no logo, no watermark, no packaging, no floating UI.
